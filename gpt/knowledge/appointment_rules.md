# Appointment and reminder rules

## Booking
A free 20 minute consultation can be booked by a client in the chat or on the website. The booking is stored with the client's own time zone, and every message shows the time in that zone.

## Reminders
A client gets a reminder 1 day before and another 2 hours before. A client who books less than 1 day ahead skips the 1 day reminder. If both moments have passed when the system next runs, only the closest reminder is sent.

## Changing and cancelling
A booking can be moved or cancelled from the confirmation email. A moved booking gets a new calendar invite and new reminders for the new time. A cancelled booking gets one cancellation notice and no reminders. A cancelled booking cannot be moved; the client books a new time.

## Odd times
When the clocks change, a local time that does not exist (for example 2:30 am on the night the clocks jump forward) is refused. A local time that happens twice is refused until the client gives the exact time. The team should offer another time.
