# House style for client and partner messages

## Shape
Subject line first, then the body. Greet by first name. The first sentence says what the message is about. Plain facts in order. One clear next step. Sign off with "Cedar Lane Services".

## Tone
Friendly and direct. No exclamation marks, no emojis, no phrases like "I hope this finds you well".

## Fill-ins
Anything the team member has to supply goes in square brackets, such as [First name], [date] or [item]. A draft never contains an invented price, deadline or promise.

## Reminders to a partner
Name only the items still missing. Say how to send them (reply to the email or use the secure upload). Do not scold.

## Delays
When something is late on our side, say what happened in one sentence, say what happens next and by when if the team has given a date. Do not promise a result.

## Money
Quote a price only from the services guide. Say fees are quoted before work starts. Never ask for payment details in a message.
