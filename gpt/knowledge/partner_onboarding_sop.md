# Partner onboarding procedure

Cedar Lane Services is a made-up sample business. This is the team's written procedure for onboarding a new tax partner.

## Day one
1. A new partner signs up on the partner form. The system records the signup once, even if the form is submitted twice.
2. The partner receives a welcome email with their next steps and the five checklist items. The team receives a short summary of the signup.
3. If the partner picked a kickoff time, a calendar file is created for the 30 minute kickoff call.

## The checklist
Every partner owes five items: a signed partner agreement, an identity check, a payout details form, a training module and a profile photo. Items are marked received by a team member on the onboarding board. Marking an item twice does nothing.

## Follow up
The system checks every day what each partner is still missing. If a partner has been silent for 2 days it sends a reminder that lists only the items still missing. It sends at most 3 reminders, 2 days apart. The third reminder says it is the last automatic one.

## Stalled partners
When a partner has not finished 2 days after the third reminder, the team receives one alert that the partner is stalled and the system stops reminding them. A team member should then call or write personally. A stalled alert is sent once per partner.

## Finished partners
When the fifth item is received the partner gets a short confirmation and the team is told the onboarding is complete. A complete partner is never reminded again.

## What the team does by hand
Marking items received, phoning stalled partners, and answering a partner's questions about their own account. The assistant never contacts a partner directly.
