# Sensitive client information

## What counts
Social security numbers, tax identification numbers (EIN), bank account and routing numbers, card numbers, dates of birth and copies of identity documents.

## Rules
1. Never type or paste any of these into the chat, an email or an assistant. The chat on the website hides them before anything is stored.
2. Documents go through the secure upload link a team member sends. Team members never ask a client to email a document.
3. If a client sends one by mistake, a team member deletes the message, tells the client not to send it that way and sends the secure upload link.
4. Logs, handoff notes and summaries keep only masked values such as [SSN].
5. Only the assigned team member opens a client's documents.

## Escalation
Anything about a client's own file, refund or account is handled by a team member and never answered by the assistant.
