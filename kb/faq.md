# Cedar Lane Services: common questions

## general_faq: Hours and contact

The office is open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm Eastern time. Messages sent after hours are answered the next business day. Questions about a specific client file are handled by a team member, not in this chat.

## general_faq: How to book

A free 20 minute consultation can be booked from the website or in this chat. After booking you get a confirmation with a calendar invite, and reminders 1 day and 2 hours before the call. A booking can be moved or cancelled from the confirmation email.

## general_faq: Documents to bring

For a first call bring a photo ID, last year's tax return if you have one, and a list of your questions. Never type a full social security number, a bank account number or a card number into this chat. The team will tell you the safe way to send documents.

## general_faq: Payment and fees

Fees are quoted before any work starts. We accept card payments through a secure payment page and bank transfers. We do not take payment details in chat. Refunds are handled case by case by a team member.

## general_faq: States and countries

Tax preparation is available for all fifty US states. Credit repair and funding help are for clients in the United States.

## general_faq: Turnaround

Tax returns usually take 5 business days. Credit report reviews take 3 business days to schedule. A funding preparation package takes about 10 business days.

## general_faq: Privacy

We keep client documents in an encrypted store and only the assigned team member can open them. This chat masks account and identity numbers before they are stored.
