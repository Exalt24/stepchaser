# Cedar Lane Services: what we offer

Cedar Lane Services is a made-up sample business used to build and test this kit. Every figure below is invented.

## tax_preparation: Tax preparation

We prepare personal and small business tax returns. A simple personal return starts at $149. A return with a small business or rental income starts at $329. Most returns are finished within 5 business days after we receive every document. We do not give legal advice. Bring last year's return, income forms and a photo ID to the first appointment.

## tax_software_training: Tax software and training

We sell a tax preparation software licence for small offices at $59 per month, and a live training course for new preparers. The course runs 6 weeks, with 2 sessions per week, and costs $450. A recording of each session is available for 30 days. Training covers software setup, interviewing a client and checking a return before it is filed.

## credit_repair: Credit repair

Credit repair is a monthly plan at $89 per month. We review a client's credit reports, help dispute items that look wrong and explain the steps to rebuild a score. We cannot promise a score or a result, and a dispute can take 30 to 45 days to be answered. We never ask for card numbers or bank logins in chat.

## business_funding: Business funding

We help owners prepare a funding application: a business plan summary, the documents a lender asks for and a shortlist of lenders that fit. The preparation package is $750. We do not lend money and we cannot promise an approval. A business should have been operating for at least 6 months to be a good fit.

## digital_products: Digital products

Digital products are templates and guides that can be downloaded right away: a tax organiser spreadsheet for $19, a client intake form pack for $29 and a small business bookkeeping guide for $39. A purchase comes with a download link by email, and updates for 12 months.

## partner_program: Tax partner program

Tax professionals can join as partners. After signing up they receive a welcome email and a checklist: a signed partner agreement, an identity check, a payout details form, a training module and a profile photo. The team follows up on anything still missing. Partners earn a share of fees from clients they refer.
